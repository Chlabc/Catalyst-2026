# Video Pitch Script — MenstraMission (~4 min)

## 1. Hook (15s)
The shared story that started this: struggling through a first period with
no clear, judgment-free place to go for answers.

## 2. Credit + blueprint (20s)
"We built on [Product-thon team name]'s MenstraMission design — a
first-point-of-contact site for pre-teens learning about menstruation,
based on their questionnaire and interview research."

## 3. What we kept and why (30s)
- The biome/interactive-scenario learning concept
- The safe-space, first-point-of-contact framing

## 4. What we changed and why (60s) — walk the divergence log
- Broadened audience slightly (pre-teen–late teen, parents/educators)
- Calmer, more direct visual tone
- Added tracker, quiz, product library
- Cut general-health scope to stay defensible and finishable

## 5. Live demo (90s)
Scenario module → product library → quiz → tracker.

## 6. Close (15s)
What's next: sample-reward system, multilingual support (both already in
the original's own future vision — cite continuity, don't invent new scope).
